Keeping pace without AI? The AI-free caucus on arXiv

Version 1, 11 October 202612 pagesPDFInfographic

The Association for Human Mathematics lists 809 mathematicians, and 532 of them, the AI-free caucus, state that they do not use AI models in their research. Here I compare their arXiv output with that of comparable mathematicians: all other authors of arXiv mathematics papers, reweighted to the same fields, career stage and earlier output. The analysis plan was fixed before any result was computed. Hover, tap or use the arrow keys on a chart to read the numbers.

Read with care

  • Only groups are compared. Nothing here says anything about a single person, and a jump in one author's output has many ordinary reasons.
  • Membership is a choice, so a difference between the groups is not an effect of avoiding AI. The other AHM members, for example, grew faster than comparable mathematicians.
  • The summer of 2026 is three months of data, and its interval only just excludes no difference.
  • Authors are recognized by their names, which merges people with the same name. Details, all further questions and the limits are in the paper.
